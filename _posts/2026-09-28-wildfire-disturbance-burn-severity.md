## Project Overview

This project analyzes wildfire disturbance and burn severity using Google Earth Engine and Landsat imagery in Beaver, Utah.

## Methods

I used Landsat imagery to examine changes in vegetation and land surface conditions before and after wildfire. The analysis included vegetation indices, land surface temperature, and differenced Normalized Burn Ratio (dNBR).

The burn severity analysis classified areas into different levels of disturbance based on dNBR values.

## Tools

- Google Earth Engine
- Landsat
- dNBR
- NDVI
- Land Surface Temperature
- Remote sensing

## Results

The analysis shows the spatial pattern of wildfire disturbance and burn severity across the study area near Beaver, Utah.

I am an FAA Part 107 certified drone pilot with experience operating multirotor and fixed-wing aircraft. I use drones to capture aerial photography and video of landscapes, natural areas, and other locations.

This gallery showcases aerial photographs from Spiral Jetty at the Great Salt Lake, a local park, and a recreation area in Montana.
